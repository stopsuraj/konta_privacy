Privacy Policy for KONTA

Last updated: November 11, 2026

KONTA is an offline card game application developed for entertainment and gameplay.

Information We Collect

KONTA does not collect, store, or process any personal information from users.

The app does not require:

  • Account registration or login
  • Name, email address, or phone number
  • Location data
  • Contacts
  • Photos or media
  • Payment information
  • Personal files

Internet Connection

KONTA is designed to work offline. The offline games do not require an internet connection.

Local multiplayer features may use the device's local Wi-Fi or hotspot connection to allow nearby players to play together. This does not mean that personal information is collected or sent to our servers.

Data Sharing

KONTA does not sell, share, or transfer users' personal information to third parties because the app does not collect personal information.

Children's Privacy

KONTA does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If the privacy practices of KONTA change in the future, this Privacy Policy may be updated accordingly.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or KONTA, you can contact the developer through the support contact provided on the app store listing.
